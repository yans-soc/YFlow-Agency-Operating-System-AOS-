YFlow - AI Agency Operating System

Product Vision & Idea Draft

Vision

YFlow adalah platform AI Agency Operating System, bukan sekadar Project
Management. Filosofi utama: - Manual-first, AI-assisted. - Pengguna
selalu memegang kendali. - AI berperan sebagai co-pilot, advisor,
planner, dan optimizer.

Core Philosophy

Alur utama: Client Brief → AI Analysis (opsional) → Project Blueprint →
Workflow → Schedule → Execution → Learning → Optimization

Semua proses dapat dibuat dengan: 1. Manual 2. Template 3. Marketplace
4. AI Generate

Core Engines

1.  Project Engine
2.  Workflow Engine
3.  Scheduling Engine
4.  Automation Engine
5.  Knowledge Engine
6.  AI Intelligence Engine

Workflow

Workflow menjadi inti sistem, bukan Task.

Workflow terdiri dari: - Stage - Milestone - Task - Subtask -
Checklist - Dependency - Approval - Deliverables - SOP - Risk -
Automation - Timeline - Resource - Budget

Scheduling

Mendukung: - Calendar Month - Week - Day - Agenda - Timeline - Planner -
Time Blocking - Team Calendar - Company Calendar - Capacity Planning -
AI Scheduler - Smart Reschedule

Calendar Concept

Setiap kotak tanggal menjadi mini dashboard.

Menampilkan: - Jumlah task - Avatar/siluet anggota - Status ringkas -
Deadline - Meeting - Approval - AI warning - Heatmap workload -
Capacity - Milestone

Detail muncul saat tanggal diklik.

Differentiator

YFlow tidak hanya menyimpan task. YFlow menyimpan cara terbaik
menyelesaikan pekerjaan (Workflow Intelligence). Semakin banyak project
berjalan, semakin cerdas rekomendasi workflow, estimasi, penjadwalan,
dan optimasi yang dihasilkan.
